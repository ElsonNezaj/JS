☀️ Solar Energy

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity.

Solar energy uses photovoltaic technology to convert sunlight into clean electricity and reducing emissions.
Solar energy is one of the most sustainable energy sources. It reduces carbon emissions,
electricity bills and can be installed on rooftops or large solar farms worldwide

💨 Wind Energy

Wind turbines generate renewable electricity.

Wind energy harnesses airflow to generate power efficiently with minimal environmental impact.
Wind energy is clean, renewable and cost-efficient. Modern wind farms supply power to millions
of homes with minimal environmental impact.

💧 Hydro Energy

Flowing water produces renewable electricity.

Hydropower provides stable and reliable energy by using rivers and water flow systems.
Hydropower is renewable energy generated by converting the kinetic and potential energy of moving water into electricity.
It plays a key role in balancing renewable energy grids and reducing dependence on fossil fuels.
