☀️ Solar Energy

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity.

Solar energy is one of the most sustainable energy sources. It reduces carbon emissions, lowers electricity bills and can be installed on rooftops or large solar farms worldwide.

💨 Wind Energy

Wind turbines harness wind power to generate electricity.

Wind energy is clean, renewable and cost-efficient. Modern wind farms supply power to millions of homes with minimal environmental impact.

💧 Hydro Energy

Hydropower generates electricity using flowing water.

Hydropower provides stable and reliable energy. It plays a key role in balancing renewable energy grids and reducing dependence on fossil fuels.

⚛️ Nuclear Energy

Nuclear reactors generate high output energy.

Nuclear energy offers low carbon emissions and consistent power generation, supporting global energy demand with advanced safety technologies.
